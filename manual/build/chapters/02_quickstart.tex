\section{Quickstart}\label{quickstart}

The fastest way to get beats out of the box.

\draftnote{The factory SD card image is not described in the project documents. Replace
steps 1 and 2 with the real download location and folder list once the factory image exists.}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Prepare an SD card formatted \textbf{FAT32} and copy the factory image
  files to its root folder.
  \verify{Factory card image name, download location and contents.}
\item
  Insert the card with the power off, then power on. The display shows
  the start-up screen while the card is read. Start-up can take a few
  seconds because the firmware repairs and settles the card contents
  before audio begins.
\item
  Press \textbf{Play}. The step LEDs start running.
\item
  Press some of the 16 step buttons in Voice mode. Track 1 plays.
\item
  Press voice button 2 and set some more steps. Track 2 plays as well.
\item
  Move a slider to change the level of a voice.
\item
  Press the \textbf{Load/Save} mode button. The Load page opens. Choose
  \textbf{Bank} and turn the encoder to another slot; the Bank loads
  when you confirm.
\item
  Press \textbf{Performance} mode, then one of the SEQ buttons to switch
  to another Scene.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{If something is wrong}\label{if-something-is-wrong}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Silence:} check that a plug is in a MAIN or OUT2 jack, check
  the voice slider, and check the voice's output route and fader mode.
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{Img\ BAD\ s:...} at start-up:} the firmware image in
  flash failed its integrity check. Re-install the firmware; see the
  Firmware Update chapter.
\item
  \textbf{Samples show as stale:} the sample catalogue on this device is
  from an older firmware. Reinstall samples with \textbf{Load
  \textgreater{} Samples} (see the Storage chapter).
\end{itemize}
